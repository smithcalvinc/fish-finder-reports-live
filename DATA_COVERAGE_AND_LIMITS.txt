DATA COVERAGE, REPORT RANKING AND LIMITS

PUBLIC ACCESS
-------------
Only explicit official public-access evidence is included. A water appearing on
a map is not automatically treated as legally reachable by the public.

REPORT RANKING
--------------
1. Official Idaho Fish and Game reports
2. Established fishing websites and tackle-shop reports
3. Manually verified public social-media posts

FRESHNESS
---------
very_current: 0-14 days
current: 15-30 days
recent: 31-90 days
stale: more than 90 days
no_recent_public_report_found: no matching public report located

FACEBOOK
--------
The workflow does not scrape Facebook Groups. Meta removed the Groups API.
Individual public post links may be entered in public_social_reports.csv after
a human verifies that the post is public and writes a short original summary.

COPYRIGHT
---------
The database stores short summaries and source links. It does not reproduce
complete fishing reports or social-media posts.

FISH SPECIES EVIDENCE
---------------------
Every search result displays a species status. FFO lists a fish only when an
official record, survey, stocking entry, report, or exact state-agency water
page ties that fish to the named water. A statewide species list is never used
as if every fish on it occurs in every lake.

The results page routes all 50 states to their official fish-and-wildlife
source. Idaho, Montana, Utah, Colorado, Wyoming, Nevada, Oregon, Washington and
Northern California currently have deeper imported records; additional exact
records are refreshed by state-specific adapters. California's CDFW High
Mountain Lakes survey is imported as exact coordinate-level survey evidence.

State agencies do not all publish a water-specific species list for every
named lake. When no exact list has been loaded, FFO says so and links both the
official state directory and a Google query restricted to that agency's
official domains. "No exact record loaded" does not mean "no fish."

NOT EVERY WATER RECEIVES PUBLIC REPORTS
---------------------------------------
Some small ponds, creeks and remote waters may have no recent public report.
They remain listed with their verified access information and an honest
no-report status.

SAFETY
------
Report dates and source links must remain visible on the website. Anglers should
verify closures, posted signs, regulations, road conditions, water levels and
weather before traveling.
